Admissions

Join a school where excellence, discipline, and learner success come first.

Welcome to Our Admissions

Tswinga Primary School welcomes new learners who are ready to grow academically, socially, and personally in a safe, supportive, and disciplined environment.

We are committed to providing quality education that builds confidence, leadership, and future-ready learners.

Our admissions process is simple, transparent, and focused on giving every child the opportunity to succeed.

Admission Requirements

Birth Certificate

Certified copy of learner’s birth certificate is required during registration.

Parent ID Copy

Certified copies of parent or guardian identification documents.

Previous Report

Latest school report card for transferring learners where applicable.

Proof of Address

Recent proof of residence for school record purposes.

Application Process

Complete the admission form, submit all required supporting documents, and wait for confirmation from the school administration office.

Office Support

Our administration office is available to assist parents and guardians with registration guidance and application support.

How to Submit
