Leading With Excellence

We are a community school that was built in 1954 under the capable leadership of His Excellency Chief Tshishonge. Since its inception, the school has shown rapid growth and development, driven by a strong commitment to providing quality education.

Under the visionary leadership of Mrs. Tshamano A.A, the school has grown from 770 learners to over 1200 learners, supported by 33 dedicated teachers and 4 departmental heads.

We remain committed to discipline, innovation, academic excellence and preparing future-ready learners through quality education.

Academic Phases

Foundation Phase

Grade R – Grade 3 focused on literacy, numeracy and learner development.

Intermediate Phase

Grade 4 – Grade 6 building strong academic performance and leadership.

Senior Phase

Grade 7 preparing learners for secondary education and future excellence.

Our Vision

To deliver quality education by equipping learners with critical cognitive skills, honesty, compassion and a global outlook on life.

Our Mission

To develop active and creative minds spiritually, morally, intellectually and socially through partnership with all stakeholders.
